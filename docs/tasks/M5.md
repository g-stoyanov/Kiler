## Decisions for the module

Decided at the start of M5, on 2026-10-30. The rules that follow from them are in the `kiler-dotnet` skill ([core.md](../../.claude/skills/kiler-dotnet/core.md), "Templates, stashes and records"), `kiler-crypto` and the `security-reviewer` agent.

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| The scope until the exam (decided by the user) | Besides the deferred steps above: the templates are read only, also those without records. `GET /api/templates` gives them to every reader; creating and changing a template come with step 10 and the template screen after the exam (PLAN section 10), and so does the open question of PLAN section 6 on a template that has records. The general settings of step 9 get no endpoint until their screen (PLAN section 10), as the page gets guest mode from the session. The private records of the demo data come with the private records. |
| The initial templates (decided by the user) | The wizard writes the four of design 6.1 to 6.3 into `templates\` (`NewKiler`), in English, so a new Kiler and the demo data get them by one path; admins change them after the exam. A data folder that the wizard of M4 made has none, so `dev-data` is made again with the demo data. Design 6.1 and 7.7 are updated. |
| The levels of the fields of a record (decided by the user) | Beside `protected`, a record keeps in clear the keys of the fields of each group, an addition to design 5.2 (updated): `"fields": { "1": ["host", "port", "tns"], "3": ["user", "password"] }`. A reader below a level sees "🔒 requires level X" on the field itself, also on a field that its author raised, and tells an empty field from a locked one. The lists name which fields are filled at which level, never their values. For a group that the session can read, the decrypted group is the truth; `fields` only labels the groups it cannot read, so a list changed by hand shows wrong padlocks and nothing else. |
| The ids of records and stashes (decided by the user) | 8 characters of `Password.TemporaryAlphabet`, the lowercase Crockford Base32 of the temporary password (`0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz`), 40 random bits from `RandomNumberGenerator`, such as `k3x9q2mf`. A create that meets `Exists`, a document of that id there or in the trash (M2.5), takes a new id. |
| The API and the parallel M6 | M5.1 writes the API of the module into `docs/api.md`, and the later tasks follow it; a change of the API goes there first. Once M5.1 is committed and the main session has started M6, the UI of M6 can go on in a [parallel session](README.md#parallel-sessions), with the demo records from M5.5 on. |
| Delete and restore, the request of a secret, the conflict (M5.1, decided by the user) | An editor deletes a record, restores it from the trash and restores an old version only when they hold the level of every group of it (for an old version, of the current one and the old one), as a restore writes the groups of every level. "Show" and "copy" are one request that says which it is, for the audit after the exam. The 409 of a conflict carries the record as it is now. The details are in `docs/api.md`, "Decided in M5.1"; design 7.4 is updated. |

## Tasks

- [x] **M5.1 The API contract**
- [ ] **M5.2 Templates**
  - What (steps 1 and 2):
    - the template model in Kiler.Core, read from `templates\<id>.json` and checked (core.md, "Models"): a `name`, an `icon`, and `fields`, each with a `key` unique in the template and none of the header (design 6.1), a `label`, a `type` of design 6.4, and the settings of design 6.5 where they belong (`options` for `select`; `language`, `dialect` or `languages` for `code`; `refTemplate` for `ref`; `levelByEnv` only beside a field `env` of type `select` at level 0, with keys among its options). A template that does not fit is invalid, with a reason;
    - the four initial templates of design 6.1 to 6.3, in English, written by the wizard (the decisions);
    - the templates in memory, following the loads as the team does (core.md, "The team");
    - `GET /api/templates` (`docs/api.md`).
  - Tests: each rule of the check, with a template that fits it and one that does not; the four initial templates fit; the wizard writes them, and the demo data has them; a template changed, damaged or gone on the share is followed; the endpoint for a guest in guest mode, a user, and a guest without guest mode (403).
  - Done: the tests of Kiler.Core, Kiler.App and Kiler.DemoData pass; a new Kiler made by the wizard of `npm run dev` has the four templates.
  - From earlier tasks:
    - (M5.1) `GET /api/levels` comes with the templates, for the names of the levels in the form. The check of a template also refuses a `secret` below level 1 and a `secret` with `list` or `filter`. The answers are in `docs/api.md` ("Template", `GET /api/templates`, `GET /api/levels`).
- [ ] **M5.3 Stashes and records in memory**
  - What (steps 3, 5 and 12, the reading):
    - the stash model of design 6.6: a `name`, the dates and authors. The names are unique without regard to case, but two of one name, made at the same moment, are both read (an admin merges them after the exam);
    - the record model of design 5.2 with `fields`: `template`, the dates and authors, `public` (the stash path of 1 to 4 ids without gaps or repeats, `name`, `description`, `tags`, and the fields of level 0), `protected` (a group per level from 1) and `fields`. A record that does not fit is invalid, with a reason;
    - the records as the session reads them, in memory: each group that the session holds, decrypted (`Session.Use`) without the values of the `secret` fields, and the fields of the other groups with their level; a guest reads level 0. They follow the loads and are dropped at a lock;
    - the stashes in memory, for every reader.
  - Tests: each model reads what it writes, and the cases that do not fit; a group that does not decrypt (damaged, or moved from another record or level) leaves the rest of the record; the guest and a user of each level read what they hold, and no `secret` value is kept; a lock drops the records; a record new, changed and gone on the share is followed.
  - Done: the tests of Kiler.Core pass.
  - From earlier tasks:
    - (M4.5b) the records follow `CurrentSession.Changed`, whose handler does not throw (core.md, "Keys and the session"); if it can, add the guard of the M4.5b finding in M4.md, "Left for after the exam".
    - (M2.8b) `LoadResult.Changed` gives a document again only when its bytes or its owner changed, so a change outside Kiler of the same size can come up to 5 minutes late (the M2.8b line in M2.md).
    - (M5.1) the record in memory gives each field as one of the kinds of `docs/api.md` ("Field"): of a `secret` it keeps only whether it has a value. A record whose template is missing or does not fit keeps only its header, and a record whose `fields` names other levels than `protected` does not fit ("Record" there).
- [ ] **M5.4 Creating a record and a stash**
  - What (steps 3, 4 and 12, in Kiler.Core):
    - a new record: an id of the decisions; the dates and authors from the exe, in UTC (`TimeProvider`); a stash path of 1 to 4 stashes that the copy has, without gaps or repeats; a name; the description; the tags; the fields of its template, each checked by its type (a `select` among its options, a `number`, a `date`, a `url`, a `code` with its language, a `ref` to a record of its `refTemplate`) and `required`;
    - the level of each field (design 5.2, 6.5 and 7.4): the template's, or its `levelByEnv` by the record's `env`, or higher when the author raises it; the stashes, the name, the tags and `env` stay at 0 (design 6.1 and 6.6). A field above the author's level is refused. Each group is encrypted with its level (core.md, "Templates, stashes and records") and listed in `fields`;
    - a new stash: a name that is not blank and is unique without regard to case among the stashes of the copy;
    - written through the copy (core.md, "Calling the layer").
  - Tests: a record reads back as it was written, with a field of each type; `levelByEnv` puts the password of `prod` at its level; a raised description; a field above the author's level refused; a path with a gap, a repeat, a fifth stash or an unknown stash refused; a value that does not fit its type, and a required field left out, refused; a `ref` to a record of another template or to one that is not there; an id that meets `Exists` takes a new one; a new stash, and a name that is there in another case.
  - Done: the tests of Kiler.Core pass.
  - From earlier tasks:
    - (M5.1) the default level of a field and a raise, the values of each type, and the trimmed name and tags are in `docs/api.md` ("Levels of a field", "Values by type", `POST /api/records`).

- [ ] **M5.6 Search, filters and the tree**
  - What (steps 7 and 12, and the search of step 9), over the records of M5.3:
    - the search text of each record (design 11): the name, the description, the tags, the names of its stashes and its readable fields, never a `secret`; every word, without regard to case, in Cyrillic and in Latin;
    - the filters: the stash path, the template, the tags (a record has every selected tag) and the fields with `filter`;
    - the tree of stashes with counters, built from the records that match (design 6.6), and the facets with counters;

  - Tests: words in Cyrillic and in Latin, in any case; a password is never found; the tag filter; a field filter; the tree and its counters under a node; 2000 records of the volume mode of M5.5, with the time; the endpoint for each kind of caller.
  - Done: the tests of Kiler.Core and Kiler.App pass.
- [ ] **M5.7 Reading a record and its secrets**
  - What (steps 5, 6 and 8, and the reading of step 9):
    - `GET /api/records/{id}`: the header with the stash path, and the fields in the order of the template: a value, "🔒 requires level X" with the name of the level, or a `secret` without its value;
    - the `ref` of a field: the title and the id of its record, or that the record has gone (design 6.3);
    - the request of a `secret` (`docs/api.md`): it decrypts the group again and answers one value of a level that the session holds, kept nowhere; the audit of Prod comes after the exam (PLAN section 10).
  - Tests: a user of each level reads their fields and the padlocks of the rest, a raised field too; a `secret` is in no answer of the list or the record; the request of a secret refuses a field above the level, a field that is not a `secret`, an unknown record or field, and the guest; a `ref` to a record that has gone; a locked session.
  - Done: the tests of Kiler.Core and Kiler.App pass.
- [ ] **M5.8 Editing a record**
  - What (steps 3 and 4, in Kiler.Core):
    - an edit over the version that the editor read: a newer version on the share is a conflict, with the record as it is now (core.md, "Calling the layer", for a slot in the way);
    - the partial edit of design 5.2: the groups above the editor's level stay as they are, with their lists in `fields`;
    - a field moved across a level that the editor does not hold is refused, also through a change of `env` (`levelByEnv`) or a raise;
    - `updatedAt` and `updatedBy` from the exe; `createdAt` and `createdBy` never change.
  - Tests: a partial edit keeps the group above the editor's level as it was, byte for byte; an editor cannot change `env` so that the password goes above their level, nor bring it down from there; a raise and a lowering within the editor's levels; a conflict; the dates and the authors.
  - Done: the tests of Kiler.Core pass.
  - From earlier tasks:
    - (M5.1) the edit holds the record at the levels of the editor: a field left out has no value, but a `secret` left out keeps its value; a group of the editor's levels that does not decrypt is not written over (`docs/api.md`, `PUT /api/records/{id}`).
