# M5. Templates, records and search

The steps are in [PLAN.md, M5](../PLAN.md#m5-templates-records-and-search). How to work on the tasks is described in [README.md](README.md). The done tasks, with all their notes, are in [log/M5.md](log/M5.md).

Until the exam, steps 10 and 11 are deferred, with what serves the private records in the other steps, the audit of step 6, and renaming, merging and deleting stashes (steps 9 and 12; creating a stash stays) ([PLAN.md, section 10](../PLAN.md#10-deferred-until-after-the-exam)). The tasks follow the [light cycle](README.md#light-cycle-until-the-exam): `security-reviewer` makes its one pass of the tasks that decrypt, encrypt or show the fields of a record, or decide who may write them: M5.3, M5.4, M5.7, M5.8, M5.9, M5.10 and M5.11.

## Decisions for the module

Decided at the start of M5, on 2026-10-30. The rules that follow from them are in the `kiler-dotnet` skill ([core.md](../../.claude/skills/kiler-dotnet/core.md), "Templates, stashes and records"), `kiler-crypto` and the `security-reviewer` agent.

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| The scope until the exam (decided by the user) | Besides the deferred steps above: the templates are read only, also those without records. `GET /api/templates` gives them to every reader; creating and changing a template come with step 10 and the template screen after the exam (PLAN section 10), and so does the open question of PLAN section 6 on a template that has records. The general settings of step 9 get no endpoint until their screen (PLAN section 10), as the page gets guest mode from the session. The private records of the demo data come with the private records. |
| The initial templates (decided by the user) | The wizard writes the four of design 6.1 to 6.3 into `templates\` (`NewKiler`), in English, so a new Kiler and the demo data get them by one path; admins change them after the exam. A data folder that the wizard of M4 made has none, so `dev-data` is made again with the demo data. Design 6.1 and 7.7 are updated. |
| The levels of the fields of a record (decided by the user) | Beside `protected`, a record keeps in clear the keys of the fields of each group, an addition to design 5.2 (updated): `"fields": { "1": ["host", "port", "tns"], "3": ["user", "password"] }`. A reader below a level sees "🔒 requires level X" on the field itself, also on a field that its author raised, and tells an empty field from a locked one. The lists name which fields are filled at which level, never their values. For a group that the session can read, the decrypted group is the truth; `fields` only labels the groups it cannot read, so a list changed by hand shows wrong padlocks and nothing else. |
| The ids of records and stashes (decided by the user) | 8 characters of `Password.TemporaryAlphabet`, the lowercase Crockford Base32 of the temporary password (`0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz`), 40 random bits from `RandomNumberGenerator`, such as `k3x9q2mf`. A create that meets `Exists`, a document of that id there or in the trash (M2.5), takes a new id. |
| The API and the parallel M6 | M5.1 writes the API of the module into `docs/api.md`, and the later tasks follow it; a change of the API goes there first. Once M5.1 is committed and the main session has started M6, the UI of M6 can go on in a [parallel session](README.md#parallel-sessions), with the demo records from M5.5 on. |
| Delete and restore, the request of a secret, the conflict (M5.1, decided by the user) | An editor deletes a record, restores it from the trash and restores an old version only when they hold the level of every group of it (for an old version, of the current one and the old one), as a restore writes the groups of every level. "Show" and "copy" are one request that says which it is, for the audit after the exam. The 409 of a conflict carries the record as it is now. The details are in `docs/api.md`, "Decided in M5.1"; design 7.4 is updated. |
