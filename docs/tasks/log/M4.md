- [ ] **M4.1 Models and the account**
  - What (PLAN M4 step 1):
    - the models of `levels.json`, `settings.json` and `users\<account>.json` in Kiler.Core, as the decisions say. Each is read from a `Document` and written to its content with a case-sensitive, source-generated JSON context, and checked: a document that does not fit (a missing name, a level out of order or twice, a name of `keys` that is not the digits of a level, values out of their limits) is invalid, with a reason, not an exception;
    - the current account behind an interface of Kiler.Core: the Windows user name without the domain, in lowercase, checked with `StorageName.Check`. Its implementation is in Kiler.App, with `--account <name>` in `StartOptions` and `--help`, and the tests use a fake.
  - Tests: each model reads what it writes; each invalid case above, and a name in another case; the account without the domain and in lowercase, a name that fails the check refused; `--account` parsed, and refused without a name or with a bad one.
  - Done: the tests of Kiler.Core and Kiler.App pass.
  - Note: decided by the user in this task. A name in `keys` of a user file or of `recovery` that is not the one digit of a level, `"1"` to the top level, makes the document invalid; the M3.3 note under this task said it is left out, and the task said invalid. `displayName` of 1 to 100 characters, not blank; `autoLockMinutes` from 1 to 1440; a level name also not blank. The decisions table of M4.md has them.
  - Note: the code. Kiler.Core: `CoreJsonContext` with the JSON types, every member nullable (`ModelJson.cs`), `KeySets` (the salt and keys of a locked set), `LevelsFile` with `LevelEntry`, `SettingsFile`, `UserFile`, and `ICurrentAccount` with `AccountName.From` (`CurrentAccount.cs`); `InternalsVisibleTo Kiler.Core.Tests` for the `TryRead(DocumentKey, JsonObject, …)` of the tests. Kiler.App: `WindowsAccount` (the name of the token through `WindowsIdentity`, not the `USERNAME` variable), not in DI until M4.4; `--account` in `StartOptions` through the same `AccountName.From` (domain dropped, lowercase), refused with `--diag`, passed on to the local copy; "--account is ignored." when a Kiler already runs. The rules are in `core.md`, "Models", and `SKILL.md`, "Windows parts".
  - Note: choices of the task, kept by the code review. A missing `check` from level 1 is valid and null, so only its level fails to verify (the M4.4 test); a check that is not a string, or a check at level 0, makes `levels.json` invalid. `salt` and `keys` are required in a user file (`{}` is valid) and `recovery` in `levels.json`, whose keys may cover only some of the levels. A member that a model does not know is skipped and left out of a write. The models keep no version. Only ASCII letters are made lowercase (`Ascii.ToLower`), as the lowercase of .NET makes the Kelvin sign `k`.
  - Note: the checks. `format.ps1`, `build.ps1` and `test.ps1 -NoBuild` passed: 1814 tests, 1789 passed, 25 skipped (the smoke tests and the Debug-only test); line coverage 87.5 %, branch coverage 91.1 %. `publish.ps1` and `smoke.ps1` were not run, as nothing that only the published exe shows changed. Each guard was broken alone (27 breaks, a script in the background), and each time a test of its class failed.
  - Note: the code review, one pass (light cycle): no critical or major finding. Seven minor ones are under "Left for after the exam" in M4.md. The two notes that the review found stale were fixed: the M3.2 note under M4.4 and the rule of `kiler-crypto` on a check that is not a string.
